# Haley's client-email voice — reference for all drafted emails

The exemplar is Haley's July 2026 monthly email to Roofing Force (sent 2026-08-03).
Match this, then turn the warmth up ~10% — she felt her own version was slightly formal.

## The rules

1. SHORT. 3-4 small paragraphs. The report carries the numbers; the email carries the
   relationship. At most ONE number, and only if it's the headline of the month.
2. One personal, human moment per email, grounded in something that really happened
   ("don't be surprised when his acting career takes off"). Never invent one.
3. Plain, optimistic, momentum language: "solid strides," "on the up & up." One or two
   exclamation points total, no more.
4. Always invite visibility: some version of "anything else you'd like visibility into."
5. First names. Contractions. Friendlier than formal — if it reads stiff, loosen it.
6. NEVER sounds like AI: no "I hope this email finds you well," no bullet lists, no
   "furthermore/additionally," no restating the report's sections, no corporate filler.
7. Forward-looking close — what we're excited about next month, one sentence.
8. Signature, exactly:
   Best,
   Haley Knapp
   541-915-2626
   EaveSide.com
9. Always a DRAFT for Haley to review. Never send.
